Past Sessions & Activities

Our weekly sessions are where the magic happens — students come together to learn, build, and share. While our full schedule is available to members through the chapter dashboard, here you'll find highlights from our most impactful and exciting sessions.

What Happens in a Session?

We’re more than just a coding club — we’re a launchpad for learning, building, and making new friends. As a member, you'll have

  • Fun warm-ups or coding challenges
  • A focused workshop or open build time
  • Group discussions and peer collaboration
  • Showcases of what members created

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced coder, there's always something to explore.
Note: members can view the upcoming sessions schedule on the chapter dashboard
