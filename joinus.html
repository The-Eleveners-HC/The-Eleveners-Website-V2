Become a Member of Our Hack Club!

Joining The Eleveners Hack Club means joining a community of curious, creative, and passionate students who build cool things together. Whether you’re brand new to coding or already building projects, there’s a place here for you.

Why Join?

We’re more than just a coding club — we’re a launchpad for learning, building, and making new friends. As a member, you’ll:

Take part in coding workshops, challenges, and hackathons
Build real-world projects with a supportive team
Collaborate with others and share your ideas
Learn new tools, languages, and technologies
Attend weekly hands-on sessions

Who’s Eligible?

Our club is open to all students who are 13-18 years old! No experience is required, just bring your curiosity and motivation to learn.

Ready to Get Involved?

Follow these simple steps to join our club:

  1. Make a Hack Club account and authorize!
  2. Jump on board to our club dashboard!
  3. Join the Hack Club Slack community!
  4. Access our Slack's club channel to stay up-to-date with our latest news.
  5. Check out our meeting schedule on the Session page.
  6. Show up, code, and build cool stuff with us!

Talk to Us!

Have questions about the club, our sessions, or how to get started? Message a club leader on Slack or visit one of our meetings — we're happy to help you get started.

Follow us on social media to stay updated on all our activities and events!
